Building the infrastructure of location.

GEOSPATIAL FRAMEWORK · ADDRESSING · IDENTITY

Reliable addressing is fundamental public infrastructure. It enables governments to locate and service properties, emergency services to respond rapidly, businesses to reach customers, and citizens to access vital civic services.

FRAMEWORK FLEXIBILITY

One framework.
Different local realities.

Every state has different geography, institutional traditions, existing data systems, and policy priorities. SAIP is structured as an adaptable programme framework rather than a rigid one-size-fits-all product.

IGSL works with state institutions to configure the programme around local statutory requirements while maintaining the rigorous technical architecture required for a sustainable location system.
