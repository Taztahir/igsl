ABOUT IGSL INCORPORATED 2006 · NIGERIA

Built around complex problems.

Inspired Global Systems Limited was incorporated in Nigeria in 2006 to provide technology and systems solutions to public and private institutions.

Two decades later, IGSL has evolved into an integrated technology, infrastructure, programme-delivery and strategic-solutions company. We bring together technology specialists, programme managers, advisers, institutional stakeholders and specialist delivery partners to take complex initiatives from concept through implementation and operation.

Institutional & Public Delivery
OUR PHILOSOPHY

Technology is only valuable when it works beyond the technology.

Successful systems depend upon more than software. They require the deliberate alignment of people, process, technology, infrastructure, governance and economics.

IGSL therefore approaches projects as complete operating environments rather than isolated technology deployments.

HOW WE WORK

A structured lifecycle for complex deployments.

OUR OPERATING MODEL

Specialist project teams.
