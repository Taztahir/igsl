STATE ADDRESSING & IDENTITY PROGRAMME

SAIP

Public digital infrastructure and location intelligence for modern government.

A comprehensive, state-level programme framework combining physical addressing, geospatial information, digital systems, and institutional processes.

Currently deployed for a state government in Southern Nigeria, SAIP demonstrates how addressing evolves from mere street signage into persistent, foundational digital public infrastructure that unlocks property valuation, civic revenue, public safety, and financial inclusion.
